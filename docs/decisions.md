## How to use this register

These questions are deliberately open except where a row is explicitly marked accepted. The user's Milestones 0–3 completion request authorizes conservative documented implementation rules when the sources are incomplete. [ADR 0009](adr/0009-milestone-3-accounting-working-rules.md) now records working resolutions for D02/03/04/06/07/08/17/22: exact prices and units, immutable measured lots, nominal identities, separate custody, verified manual linkage, one-unit obligations, current-day compensation and safe practice. These authorize implementation without fabricating a shop-domain or legal signature. Existing accepted decisions and external acceptance gaps remain intact. Record later policy changes as ADRs with their accounting and migration impact.

## Decision record template

Create docs/adr/NNNN-short-title.md with:
- Status: proposed, accepted, superseded, or rejected.
- Date and owners.
- Context, source requirement IDs, and a concrete shop example.
- Options considered and consequences for money, grams, UX, security and migration.
- Decision and why.
- Schema/API impact, old-client compatibility and rollback/compensation.
- Acceptance tests and UI evidence.
- Links to the implementation and any superseding decision.

## First decision workshop

D01 and the local-source baseline in D27 are accepted. Prioritize D02 through D12, D23 through D26, and the agreed opening-balance example before building the first financial migration. The Milestone 2 opening slice may proceed under the user-authorized working defaults in [ADR 0004](adr/0004-opening-balance-working-defaults.md); that pointer does not accept D02, D03, D04, D08, or G0 and does not fill the blank approval rows. A later annex comparison is a contract-record task and does not block the agreed working scope. Bring sample paper-ledger entries for one normal sale, a split-tender sale, a financed purchase with partial scrap recognition, a trader receipt recognized later, a repair handover, a partial debt settlement, and a day closed after midnight. Write expected cash by method, grams by karat, count, ownership/custody and liabilities after each example. Approve a specific three-party financier case. The team should not infer these values from screen mockups alone.

Resolve D14, D15 and D20 before production data or subscriptions are enabled; D13 is accepted. Retention, deletion and account ownership require stakeholder and legal review. The engineering documents can specify a safe implementation pattern but cannot settle commercial or legal meaning on their own.
