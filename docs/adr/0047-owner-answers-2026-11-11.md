## Context

The 2026-11-11 emulator pass left twelve owner decisions. The owner answered all of them on 2026-11-11. This record locks the answers and states how each is implemented.

## Decision

D1 (O1). When a sale takes every remaining piece of a named item at a weight different from the recorded one, the item's whole recorded weight leaves stock. The difference is booked on the sale as a `correction` lot movement with a matching `adjustment_gold_clearing` journal entry, so no other item's stock pays for it and no weight is left without pieces. Before confirming, the sale review shows an information banner: «آخر قطعة من «X» عيار K — وزنها المسجّل … وسيُخصم كاملًا. الفرق … يُسجَّل تسوية مخزون». An edit or delete of that sale restores the recorded weight: the trade-change reversal puts the correction back on the returned lot. A customer's real return keeps the weight actually sold. Partial sales (pieces remain) are unchanged.

D2. Keep the M1 split: confirmed operations that change cash or stock get a full outcome screen with the server-confirmed figures; non-financial saves (notes, repair and customer status, settings) show a toast and stay on the record.

D3 (U19). The admin sign-in says why it failed. A valid account without platform-admin access sees «هذا الحساب ليس له صلاحية الإدارة. سجّل الدخول بحساب مدير المنصة.». Wrong credentials, unconfirmed email, rate limit and connection failures have their own messages.

D4. «المساعدة» in «المزيد» stays. The owner asked to do what was requested: the requirements include the Help center (HELP-01, [ADR 0031](0031-help-content-and-announcements.md)), and the 2026-10-05 feedback removed guides, tours, practice entry points and help cards only. No guide or help card is shown anywhere.

D5 (O3). A shop whose logo predates automatic application gets it on its invoice once: on the owner's workspace, if the shop has an image and no invoice appearance was ever saved, the app applies that image as the invoice logo through the normal reviewed, idempotent appearance save. An appearance the owner already saved is never changed.

D6 (O4). Karats appear in the owner order 18, 21, 22, 14 (24 between 21 and 22 where it exists) in the sold and bought weight sheets, as in the choice lists.

D7 (O4). The edit screen shows weights with two decimals like new entry. A stored third decimal stays visible so a recorded weight is never rounded silently. Prices show grouped («25,000»).

D8 (U8). Cards and the operation detail label the staff member «المسجِّل» on purchases and every non-sale entry; sales keep «اسم البائع».

D9 (U2). The ⚡ quick-actions button keeps its current position.

D10 (U20). «تحويل الكسر إلى مخزون» shows a result page like sale and purchase, and its weight accepts two decimals.

D11 (U12). On repair handover, «تحصيل الكل» fills one payment line with the final fee, in cash when cash is available, and follows the fee while it is edited.

D12 (U3). The sale and purchase review shows each line's price when «سعر لكل صنف» is on, and the customer phone.
